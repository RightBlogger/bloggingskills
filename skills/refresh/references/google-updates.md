# Google updates to check a decline against

Collected October 2026. When a post's drop starts within a week or two of an update
window, name the update in the diagnosis. A core update hitting the post points to
quality and information gain; a spam update hitting the site points to a policy
problem, which editing one post won't fix. Re-check the Search Status Dashboard for
anything newer: status.search.google.com.

## 2026

| Dates | Update | What it means for a blog |
|-------|--------|--------------------------|
| Feb 5 | Discover core update (US English first) | Favors original, in-depth, timely posts and topic-level expertise; cuts clickbait and sensational headlines. A Discover-only drop points here. |
| Mar 24 | Spam update (about 19.5 hours) | Enforcement of the existing policies: scaled content abuse, site reputation abuse, expired domain abuse. |
| Mar 27 to Apr 8 | Core update | No named target. Google says it surfaces "relevant, satisfying content." |
| May 21 to about Jun 2 | Core update | Same as above. |
| Jun 15 | Back button hijacking enforced | A spam violation since 2026-04-13. It can come from ad or script code, not the post. |
| Jun 24 to 26 | Spam update | No new policy named. |
| Aug 18 to 21 | Spam update | No new policy named. |
| Aug 31 | Generative AI opt-out toggle global | A site that opted out of AI features gets no AI Overview, AI Mode, or AI-in-Discover traffic. |
| Sep 24 to Oct 8 | Spam update | No new policy named. |
